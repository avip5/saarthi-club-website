Saarthi is the official First-Year Student Council of VIT Pune.

Founded by seniors who once stood in your shoes, Saarthi was born to make your transition smoother

How We Support First-Year Students

  • Provide academic guidance and support
  • Organize orientation programs for freshers
  • Facilitate peer mentoring programs
  • Conduct skill development workshops
  • Create networking opportunities
  • Support personal and professional growth
